When teams usually come to us

How we engage

A product needs to get built

You have the idea, the market, or the internal need — but need a team to shape it, design it, and get it into users' hands.

Customer or internal UX isn't meeting the standard

The portal, dashboard, storefront, or internal tool works on paper, but it is confusing, slow, or not built around how people actually use it.

A workflow needs to be optimized and automated

Teams are relying on manual steps, disconnected tools, or too many check-ins to keep important work moving.

A system worked before, but does not scale now

The software got the team this far, but new users, workflows, data, or business rules are making it harder to maintain and harder to move quickly.

Manual data extraction needs streamlining

Pulling structured signals out of systems, documents, and feeds often means one-offs, brittle scripts, or repeated reconciliation before reporting and workflows can scale.

Leadership needs a grounded technical decision

Before committing more budget, headcount, or months of work, the team needs to know what to fix, what to build, and what to leave alone.

Engagement models

Choose your package

Maestro

2–4 weeks

Product Strategy & Build Blueprint

Best for

Founders and early teams with an idea, pitch, prototype, or product concept that needs clarity before development

Outcome

A clear build blueprint and a product strategy plan

Includes

  • Product discovery and ICP definition
  • MVP scope and feature prioritization
  • Technical feasibility and AI opportunity review
  • Build roadmap with clear milestones
  • Market positioning and pitch-ready product narrative

Forge

4+ weeks

MVP & Product Development Sprint

Best for

Teams ready to create a v1, rebuild a weak prototype, or turn a validated idea into working software

Outcome

A working MVP or product release that is supported by clear architecture, testing, documentation, and next steps.

Includes

  • Full product scoping and UX/UI
  • Frontend, backend, and database build
  • AI-assisted development and QA
  • Launch support and handoff planning
  • Build Readiness Review before development begins

Dynamo

6+ weeks

Software Modernization Sprint

Best for

Companies with existing software, apps, internal tools, or codebases that need hands-on improvement, modernization, or rebuild support.

Outcome

A stronger technical foundation and a clear execution plan for improving your existing software.

Includes

  • Codebase cleanup and security fixes
  • Architecture, infrastructure, and database improvements
  • Product experience review
  • Automation upgrades and AI implementation
  • Stabilization roadmap for future development
